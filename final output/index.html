EQUIPMENTS WE MONITOR

Common HVAC Emergencies We Resolve

Our qualified nationwide engineering network is equipped to diagnose and repair urgent mechanical, electrical, and refrigerant failures across all commercial HVAC plant.

Chiller Breakdowns

Critical failure or loss of cooling capacity in central water-cooled and air-cooled chillers.

Cooling System Failures

Complete cooling loss across commercial facilities, office buildings, and retail complexes.

Compressor Malfunctions

Mechanical locking, abnormal vibration, or electrical trips requiring urgent overhaul.

Refrigerant Leaks

Pressure drops, safety valve discharges, and compliance-critical leak containment.

Electrical & Control Failures

BMS communication loss, sensor faults, VSD trips, and control board errors.

Air Conditioning Breakdowns

Package unit and VRF/VRV system faults resulting in airflow shutdown or temperature spikes.
