BENEFITS OF PLANNED HVAC MAINTENANCE

Importance of Planned HVAC maintenance service

Planned HVAC maintenance is important because it stops small issues from becoming expensive breakdowns and keeps your system running the way it was designed to.

Protects your investment

Factory start-up and planned care give your equipment the best possible start and a longer life.

Keeps it safe, reliable and efficient

Technicians use the latest Carrier engineer information to check, tune and verify performance.

Prevents breakdowns

Inspections along with predictive checks catch wear and faults before they even fail.

Lowers costs

Less downtime, fewer emergency repair bills, lower energy use, and less loss of use.

Unlocks extra cover

Extended parts and labour warranty is available when a Carrier start-up is done by a Carrier Service technician.

Works for all brands

Seasonal start-up and maintenance for all HVAC equipment, regardless of manufacturer.
